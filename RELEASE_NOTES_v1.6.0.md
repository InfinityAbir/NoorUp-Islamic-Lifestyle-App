## Included changes
### Crescent Journey

**Crescent Journey** turns the Stats screen into a calm, personal view of your prayer record.

- **Your crescent, every day.** A golden crescent and five stars (Fajr to Isha) light up as you record your prayers. Tap the crescent on Stats to open the full journey.
- **Record it your way.** For any prayer choose *Completed*, *Missed* or *Not recorded*. A prayer you have not recorded is never treated as missed.
- **A sky that grows with you.** Six stages, from First Light to Eternal Light, based on your last 30 and 90 days. A quiet month never takes away what you have reached.
- **Constellations.** Six constellations to discover as you keep your record: your first full day, a first week, 30, 90, 180 days and a year.
- **Understand your consistency.** Daily history with times, weekly and monthly views with a calendar heatmap, a yearly overview, and filters for each prayer.
- **Friday Reflection.** Look back on your week every Friday: a summary, three optional questions, and one personal intention. Everything stays private on your device.
- **Gentle reminders (optional).** A quiet Friday reminder and a Monday intention reminder. The evening review now stays silent once all five prayers are recorded.
- **Your choice of motion.** Animations can be switched off, and Android's "remove animations" setting is respected.
- English and Bangla throughout.

### Tasbih widget and zikr history

- **Tasbih Counter widget (4x2).** Count your dhikr from the home screen, reset a round, jump into the app, and switch zikr with the ‹ › buttons. Counting rules are shared with the in-app counter.
- **Zikr History.** Digital Tasbih now shows today, last 7 days and all recorded counts, a 7-day chart, a per-zikr breakdown and a daily log. Counts made from the widget are included, and history is part of your backup.
- **Home Widget tab** now previews and adds all three widgets: Prayer Timeline, Daily Verse & Dua, and Tasbih Counter.

### Good to know
- Crescent Journey only reads your existing prayer records. Your Qada ledger, streak, timeline and history are unchanged.
- Nothing is shared or ranked. Your reflections are stored on your device and are included in your own encrypted backup.
- Reminders are optional and off by default. If your phone delays alarms, set NoorUp to "Unrestricted" in battery settings.

### Known limitations
- The Qada "Missed Prayers Detected" banner still counts unrecorded days (unchanged from earlier versions).
- Bangla wording for the stage names is pending a native review.
