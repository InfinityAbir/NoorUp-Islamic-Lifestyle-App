Bengali Quran pronunciation (transliteration_bn.json)
=====================================================

Source
------
Project: Khalidhassan3011/al_quran
File:    lib/src/data/full_quran_data.dart
Commit:  b2fd1a6706d475a5aefb1e93a9eae7310333dc90 (2026-02-17)
URL:     https://github.com/Khalidhassan3011/al_quran
Field:   pronunciation_bn

The source dataset states that its Quran data references alquran.cloud.
Only the Bengali pronunciation strings were extracted. Arabic text,
translations and all other fields in NoorUp continue to come from
NoorUp's existing offline Quran bundle.

Modifications
-------------
Surrounding whitespace was trimmed. No wording was added or rewritten.
Two verses are blank in the upstream source: Surah 26, verse 1 and
Surah 26, verse 227. They remain blank here, and NoorUp shows an
explicit "pronunciation not available" message instead of inventing
religious text.
