```
NoorUp is a calm, Bengali-first Islamic companion — works offline, no ads SDK.

PRAY ON TIME
• Prayer times with city presets or optional GPS, 8 calculation methods + Hanafi/Shafi Asr options
• Start–end times, countdown, Sunrise, Tahajjud and Qasr travel guidance
• Sehri-ends / Iftar-starts bar and Makruh (sunrise, zawal, sunset) windows shown
• Per-prayer alerts, Sehri/Maghrib reminders and home-screen widget
• Compare with your local masjid timetable; times are calculated, not certified

LEARN IN BANGLA
• 114-Surah index with Arabic text and Bangla + English meaning, bundled for offline reading (full text loads when you open a surah; translation source to be disclosed)
• 18 Masnoon Duas with Arabic, Bangla phonetics, meaning and references
• Hadith library with chapters, topics, grading, search and bookmarks
• One-tap copy and system-share for Duas and Hadith

PRACTICE DAILY
• Digital Tasbih with goals, Hijri calendar with event countdowns
• Zakat calculator in Vori/Taka with Nisab basis and 2.5% (8 Quranic heads explained)
• Streaks, 7-day consistency timeline and Qada ledger for missed prayers
• Offline built-in Islamic answers in Bangla and English (rule-based learning aid — consult scholars for fiqh rulings)

Private by design: no accounts, no ads SDKs. Location is optional and used on-device for prayer-time accuracy only.

Built for Bengali-speaking Muslims in Bangladesh and West Bengal. English fully supported.
```
